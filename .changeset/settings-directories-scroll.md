the members, roles and workspaces directories in settings show a few rows of cards and scroll the rest inside their own area: two cards on a narrow window, four on a mid-sized one (two by two), nine on a wide one (three by three). the search, the order and the add button stay above, outside the scroll, and a faint fade at the foot says there is more below. moving through the cards with the keyboard brings each one into view. a workspace's own page still lists its members in full.
