an organization now has roles: the owner, a manager and a member, and roles of its own ranked between the two, each a set of permissions for everything the application does, with an override on each member to switch a permission for them alone. managers give and take permissions without the owner being present. this changes the organization's format, so an organization made by an earlier version is upgraded when its owner first signs in with a connection, and until then its other members are asked to wait
