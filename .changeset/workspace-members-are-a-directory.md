a workspace's members read as a directory: search, sort and a plus sit in the tray above the cards, in place of the find-a-member field. the plus opens a sheet where you search for members not yet in the workspace, gather as many as you like on a list, and add them all with one save; a refusal says why and keeps the ones not added on the list. pressing a member's card, or edit permissions in its menu, opens a sheet of that workspace's permissions alone, which override the organization's and the role's there. the card's menu holds edit permissions and remove, and open member is gone.
