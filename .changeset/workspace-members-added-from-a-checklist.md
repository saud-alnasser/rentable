adding members to a workspace is one list. the plus opens a sheet with a search field on top and, under it, every member not yet in the workspace, each with their initials, username and role; typing narrows the same list. pressing a member, or Space on them, checks them where they stand, and the button says how many it adds. a refusal keeps the sheet open with its reason, those already added gone from the list and the rest still checked. the dropdown and the separate list of chosen members are gone.
