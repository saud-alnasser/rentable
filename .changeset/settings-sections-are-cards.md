every settings tab is a set of cards laid in a grid: two columns where the window is wide enough, one where it is not. each card carries its own title and one line saying what it is for, and the acts that sign out, disconnect, forget or delete stay last in red. updates says where it stands in words and shows the download as a progress bar; who is signed in leads the account tab with an avatar, and each machine says when it was last seen under its name. the release notes, the log folder's whole path, the sync detail and what the Turso account holds fold under their rows, closed until opened. the roles, members and workspaces lists keep their bar and cards, and workspaces lie in a grid.
