settings is one area rather than four pages. `/organization`, `/workspace` and `/account` are gone, and `/settings` holds general, account, organization and workspaces as tabs above the page, each named for what it holds and each with an address of its own so a menu or the command palette can open one directly. what a section shows is what your place in the organization allows, and somebody who is not signed in is offered general alone. the rail's workspace menu opens the workspaces section, the account menu offers settings and the way out, and the page still opens before anybody has signed in, with the language, updates and diagnostics on it
