settings is one area with seven sections rather than four pages. `/organization`, `/workspace` and `/account` are gone, and `/settings` holds general, you, members, workspaces, sync, updates and diagnostics as tabs above the page, each with an address of its own so a menu or the command palette can open one directly. what a section shows is what your place in the organization allows, and a section you have nothing to do in is not offered at all. the rail's workspace menu opens the workspaces section, the account menu offers you and settings, and the page still opens before anybody has signed in, with the language, updates and diagnostics sections on it
