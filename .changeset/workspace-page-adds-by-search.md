a workspace's page adds members by search. under its header, a field finds a member who is not in the workspace by username and puts them in at once, or says why it cannot (you may not grant workspaces, or you hold this one read only), and says so when everybody is already in. below it, the members in the workspace are member cards like the members section's, marked custom here where their access is tailored, with a note when nobody is in. each card's menu opens the member, tailors their access here (their sheet opens on this workspace's permissions), or removes them from the workspace after asking first. the switches are gone.
