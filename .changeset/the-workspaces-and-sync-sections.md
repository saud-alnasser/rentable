the workspaces section is a directory of cards, one per workspace: the name, a small dot before the one open on this machine, and how many people are in it. what you may do to it is on the card's own menu, and an act you do not hold is not there: rename the workspace you are in, members and access, and delete. members and access opens the same surface a person's card opens, read the other way round: every person in the organization at no access, full access or read only on this one workspace. delete asks once and names what goes with it, and it is the owner's. the control that makes a new workspace sits in the tray above the cards, and on an owner's machine that is not connected to the turso account the reason stands in its place. export and import sit beneath, under a heading naming the workspace they act on. what the sync section used to hold is in the organization section now: one sentence on where this machine stands with the organization on turso with a control named sync beside it, the turso account with a reconnect for a machine that holds no authority and forget turso account for one that does, and disconnect this machine at the foot; a member who is not the owner reads the standing and the disconnect. the workspace page's identity and members rows are gone with the page they were on.
