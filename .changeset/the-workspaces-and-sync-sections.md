the workspaces section is one list. a row is the workspace's name, the access you hold on it, how many people are in it and whether it is the one open on this machine, with its actions appearing when the pointer or the keyboard reaches the row: rename the workspace you are in, members and access, and delete, each offered only to somebody who may perform it. members and access opens the same panel the members list opens, read the other way round: every person in the organization at no access, full access or read only on this one workspace. delete asks once and names what goes with it, and it is the owner's. new workspace is the owner's button and, on an owner's machine that is not connected to the turso account, the sentence saying so. export and import sit beneath the list, under a heading naming the workspace they act on. the sync section is the sync status and sync now, the account's own refusal where there is one, the turso account with a reconnect for a machine that holds no authority and forget turso account for one that does, the organization's link, and disconnect this machine; a member who is not the owner reads the sync status and the disconnect. the workspace page's identity and members rows are gone with the page they were on.
