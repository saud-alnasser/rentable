the members section is one list. everybody is a row, the people who were invited and have not signed in yet included: the avatar and the username with the role beside it, the workspaces they hold as chips saying full access or read only, and on somebody who has not arrived a badge saying so with the day their link runs out. the row's actions appear when the pointer or the keyboard reaches it, and you are offered only the ones you may perform: role and permissions, workspaces and access, rename, a new link, remove, and on a pending row the link to copy again where you are the one who issued it, a new link, and revoke. role and permissions opens a panel with the three roles and a checkbox for each act, where giving somebody an act that writes another member's row is the owner's alone and says so; workspaces and access opens a panel offering each workspace at no access, full access or read only, where read only is the owner's and says so. inviting asks for a username, a role and an access per workspace, and what it shows afterwards is the organization's name, the sentence that rentable sends nothing, one link and one control to copy it. a new link and a copy link show the same panel. the separate list of pending accounts is gone.
