a sign-out of every machine holds. renaming a member, changing what they may do or removing them no longer puts their sign-out back, and a reset keeps it; a sign-out made with no connection says so and goes out at the next one. what a member may do is read off their row for every act, so taking an act away reaches a session they already have open within a few minutes rather than at their next sign-in.
