the members in the organization settings are a grid of cards: two columns, three on a wide window, one on a narrow one. each card shows the member's initials, username and role, then whether they have set a password, whether a machine is signed in on their account, how many workspaces they hold and when they joined, and marks a member with permissions of their own or one offered the organization.
