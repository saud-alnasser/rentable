money returned to a tenant can be recorded as a refund on any contract, a terminated one included: on a running contract only what was paid beyond its total cost can be refunded, and on a terminated one up to what it received, each less earlier refunds, and a refund recorded or raised above that is refused naming the most that can be returned, while a refund already recorded can always be lowered, even on a contract restored after it was refunded, and undoing a refund's edit or deletion puts back exactly what was recorded, refused only where the refunds would then be more than the contract received; a payment received can no longer be lowered or deleted where the refunds would then be more than the contract received
