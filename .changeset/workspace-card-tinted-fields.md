each workspace card in settings lays its facts as softly tinted fields, the way the member and role cards do: the people who hold it, as their initials with how many there are, across the card, then your access and the day it was created, each with its icon, a small name and the value beneath it. _open on this machine_ moves up beside the workspace's name. a workspace nobody holds says _nobody_ rather than a zero. its acts are as they were.
