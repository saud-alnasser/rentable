contracts are a grid of cards, on the contracts list, a tenant's contracts and a unit's contracts: two columns, three on a wide window, one on a narrow one. each card leads with the tenant and the status in words, then the reference, the dates and the units the contract holds, then what has been paid of what is expected, the cost per cycle, and how many payments there are once there is one.
