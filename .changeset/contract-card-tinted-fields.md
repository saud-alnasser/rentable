each contract card lays its facts as softly tinted fields, two across, like the member cards: the contract period, the contract number, the units it holds, the cost with its cycle and how many payments there are, each with its icon, a small name and the value beneath it. the ring stands at the foot beside what has been paid of what is expected. a contract with no units or no payments yet says none, muted, and the units' names are joined with a plain comma in Arabic, with no "و" running into a Latin name.
