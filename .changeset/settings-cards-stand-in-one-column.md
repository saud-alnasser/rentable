every settings tab now stands its cards one under the next in a single column. in the rows that sign out, forget, remove, disconnect or delete, only the button is red; the row's icon and name read like any other. diagnostics shows the log folder's whole path under its name with nothing folded, and opens the folder from an icon whose tooltip says so. checking for updates is an icon with its tooltip too, and the available version stays empty until a check finds one instead of saying unknown. the language and appearance card drops its footnote, and the system choice says in a tooltip that it follows your computer as it turns light or dark.
