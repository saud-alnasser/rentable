a workspace has a page of its own. opening a workspace card, or its members act, shows what the workspace is at the top (its name, whether it is open on this machine, your access, how many hold it and when it was made, with the card's acts) and below it everybody who could hold it, each as a card with a larger switch that says in words whether they are in. a switch applies at once, and one that is refused goes back and says why. back returns to the workspaces section. the members dialog is gone.
